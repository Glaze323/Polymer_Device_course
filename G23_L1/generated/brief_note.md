# G23 L1 brief note

At the exact operating point, $E_{F,op}=0.13147583$ eV, the finite-temperature band-edge count is $n_{band}=1.338755e+12$ cm^-2 and the Fermi-referenced count is $n_F=3.994505e+11$ cm^-2. Their ratio is $C=3.351490$. The $C=2$ and $C=4$ crossings occur at $E_F=0.072762$ eV and $E_F=0.157347$ eV, respectively.

$C$ increases with positive doping because $n_{band}$ accumulates all occupied states from the Dirac point to the thermal tail, whereas $n_F$ counts only the occupied states above the current Fermi level. As $E_F$ rises, the filled interval below $E_F$ grows much faster than the thermal population above it.

Using rho_square=382.774468 ohm/square gives mu_band=12179.953681 cm^2 V^-1 s^-1 and mu_corrected=40820.998829 cm^2 V^-1 s^-1. The corrected mobility is therefore 3.3515 times the band-edge estimate (235.15% higher); equivalently, the band-edge estimate is 70.16% below the corrected value. No additional factor of 10^4 was applied because the carrier density is already in cm^-2.
